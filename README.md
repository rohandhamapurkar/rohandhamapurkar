I co-founded **AlterSquare** to help founders turn complex ideas into resilient, production-grade products. I fell for programming in my second year of engineering and never looked back. Over the years I've shipped across a wide range of stacks, with a lasting affinity for **Node.js** and **Go** on the backend.
